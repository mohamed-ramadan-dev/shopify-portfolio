Feature walkthrough

Configuring a product before requesting a quote

I worked across the product gallery, variant logic and quote entry point. The page also gives the merchant reusable sections for delivery, assembly and FAQs.

  1. Choose a configuration

    The shopper selects a product family or one of its configuration groups.

  2. Resolve the variant

    The theme groups related variants and selects the group’s default option.

  3. Open the quote form

    The shopper can move from product details into the quote request.
